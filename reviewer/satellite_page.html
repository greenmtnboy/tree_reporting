Detection rings are the model's predicted crown width where the run has a crown head, otherwise allometric estimates from DBH and genus (Tallo fits in data/raw/crown_width_coefficients.csv); inventory rings are always allometric. None are measured canopies. Detections mark crown centres; inventory points mark trunks, which can sit a few metres off a tall tree's crown.
